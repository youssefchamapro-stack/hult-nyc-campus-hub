Welcome to Hult International Business School's New York City campus! This is an exclusive 4-week intensive business program designed for globally-minded students seeking to immerse themselves in one of the world's most dynamic cities.

Our curriculum combines rigorous academic learning with real-world business exposure. You'll study cutting-edge topics in innovation, entrepreneurship, and leadership while networking with industry leaders and fellow ambitious students.

Beyond the classroom, New York City is your learning playground. From Wall Street finance to Brooklyn's startup scene, from Broadway culture to world-class museums, you'll experience the full spectrum of what makes NYC the global business capital.

This hub serves as your central resource for schedules, events, photos, and essential campus information. Stay connected, stay informed, and make the most of your time in New York.
